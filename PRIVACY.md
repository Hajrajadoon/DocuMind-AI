# DocuMind AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

DocuMind AI is an app that runs inside the Anna platform. It helps people understand contracts and other documents in plain language.

## What the app processes
- **Document text.** The text you paste, or that is extracted from a PDF, Word (.docx) or TXT file you upload, is read in your browser. When you click Analyze, that text is sent to Anna's AI service (`anna.llm.complete`) so that a summary, findings and suggested wording can be generated.
- **Your review.** Your Accept, Ignore and Flag decisions and your comments stay in the app until you save a report.

## What is stored
- If you click **Save report**, the report (the analysis, your decisions and comments, and the original text for documents up to 60,000 characters) is stored in Anna's per-app storage under your account, so you can reopen it later.
- You can delete any saved report at any time from the Saved Reports section.

## What we do not do
- DocuMind AI has no servers of its own and does not run its own database.
- It does not sell your data, show ads, or use trackers.
- Exported PDF and Word files are created on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

## Your responsibility
Please avoid submitting confidential or personal information that you are not comfortable sharing with an AI service. How Anna processes data is covered by Anna's own terms and privacy policy.

## Not legal advice
DocuMind AI provides informational support only and is not legal advice. AI can make mistakes. Always verify important decisions with a qualified professional.
